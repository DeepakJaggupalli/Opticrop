# Business Requirements

## 1. Specify the Business Problem
Agricultural productivity is highly dependent on various environmental factors such as soil nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorous, Potassium), temperature, humidity, pH level, and rainfall. Farmers often lack data-driven guidance on which crops to plant based on their specific soil and climatic conditions. Planting unsuitable crops leads to reduced yields, financial losses, and inefficient use of resources (like fertilizers and water). 

## 2. Business Requirements
- **Goal:** Develop an intelligent, data-driven optimization engine (OptiCrop) that recommends the best crop for a given set of soil and environmental parameters.
- **Inputs Required:** Nitrogen (N), Phosphorous (P), Potassium (K), Temperature, Humidity, pH, and Rainfall.
- **Output:** A single, optimal crop recommendation (e.g., Rice, Maize, Apple, etc.).
- **User Interface:** A simple, intuitive web application where farmers or agricultural experts can input data and receive real-time recommendations.
- **Performance Criteria:** The machine learning model must be highly accurate (>90%) to provide reliable recommendations.

## 3. Literature Survey
Recent studies in precision agriculture demonstrate the efficacy of Machine Learning (ML) algorithms, such as K-Nearest Neighbors, Random Forests, and Logistic Regression, in predicting crop suitability. Traditional farming relies heavily on indigenous knowledge and trial-and-error, which is becoming less reliable due to climate change and unpredictable weather patterns. By using data-centric approaches, precision agriculture maximizes yields and minimizes ecological footprints. 

## 4. Social and Business Impact
- **Social Impact:** 
  - **Empowering Farmers:** Provides small-scale and large-scale farmers with accessible, expert-level agricultural advice.
  - **Food Security:** Increases overall crop yields, contributing to better food security.
- **Business Impact:** 
  - **Cost Reduction:** Optimizes the use of fertilizers and water by planting suitable crops.
  - **Revenue Growth:** Maximizes farm revenue through higher yields and reduced crop failure rates.
  - **Sustainability:** Promotes environmentally sustainable farming practices by aligning crop selection with natural soil and weather conditions.
