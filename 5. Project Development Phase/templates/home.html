About The Project

OptiCrop was conceived to bridge the gap between traditional farming practices and modern artificial intelligence. By analyzing complex agricultural datasets comprising soil nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium), pH levels, and climatic conditions (Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall), OptiCrop provides farmers with highly accurate, data-driven crop recommendations. This prevents resource wastage, maximizes yield, and promotes sustainable agriculture.
